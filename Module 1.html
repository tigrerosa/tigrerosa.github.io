Complexity Science
▪Complex systems consist ofnumerous subsystems.
▪Subsystems interact throughmultiple, nonlinear, recurrent,feedback loops.
▪Complexity resides in theindividuals’ own viewsand experiences.
▪Health care is a verycomplex system.
▪Small changes in a complexsystem may have profound,unpredictable effects on thesystem over time.
▪Assessment of the complexityshould be commensurate with thecomplexity of the system.
▪Use as an opportunity fornonlinear thinkingand innovation.
▪Similar conditions can producedissimilar results.
▪An approach that works once maynot work again.
▪Complex systems are embeddedin their own culture and history.
